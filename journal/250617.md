# 2025-06-17 Log — Weekly Zoom Meetings

## 📌 Context
We have decided to start weekly Zoom meetings with end users.  
Previously, all requests and responses were handled indirectly through a translator, and we did not work directly with the end users.

## 👥 Stakeholders
- End users in Japan (manufacturing company staff)  
- Development team in Vietnam (no Japanese language skills)  
- Translator (non-technical background)  
- Supporting developers in the Philippines (Japan side)

## ⚠️ Challenge
- Communication was previously filtered through a translator, which limited direct interaction and sometimes slowed down clarification.  
- Developers lacked opportunities to understand user needs firsthand.

## 🔧 How I Handled It
- Proposed and initiated weekly Zoom meetings to establish direct communication with end users.  
- Coordinated with the translator and Japan-side support developers to facilitate discussions.  
- Prepared agenda and structure to ensure meetings are productive and focused.

## 🛠️ Environment
- Zoom (video conferencing)  
- Project documentation tools  
- Translation support

## 📊 Outcome
- Direct communication channel established with end users.  
- Improved clarity on requirements and reduced dependency on translator-only communication.  
- Stronger collaboration between Vietnam and Japan-side teams.

## 🎯 Lessons Learned
- Direct interaction with end users accelerates understanding and reduces miscommunication.  
- Weekly cadence ensures consistent updates and feedback loops.  
- Cross-team collaboration (Vietnam + Philippines + translator) is essential for bridging language and cultural gaps.

## 📌 Next Steps
- Continue weekly Zoom meetings and refine agenda based on feedback.  
- Document key discussion points and decisions after each meeting.  
- Explore long-term solutions for bilingual communication to reduce reliance on translation.
