# 2026-01-13 Log

## 📌 Context
Meeting with JP end users. They expected developers to have a “designer’s eye” and asked UI-related questions that made the development team feel pressured. They showed screens and asked for feedback on UI, which was challenging since our role is backend development. Later, we had an internal developer meeting where advanced members who work face-to-face with end users could comment and fix directly. From VN, we faced issues harder to control. After deployment, some users could not use the new function due to cache problems.

## 👥 Stakeholders
- JP end users  
- VN backend development team  
- Internal advanced developers  
- Translator  
- IT support team  

## ⚠️ Problem
- End users expected design-level feedback from backend developers.  
- Communication gaps created confusion and fatigue.  
- Cache issues prevented users from accessing new functions.  
- Lack of logs, videos, or records made troubleshooting difficult.  
- Translator could not support in requesting detailed evidence from users.  

## 🔧 How I Handled It
- Clarified our role as backend developers while showing commitment and spirit to work.  
- Stayed professional and patient when asked UI-related questions.  
- Coordinated with internal IT teams to investigate cache issues.  
- Suggested users check their browser and clear cache, which resolved the problem.  

## 🛠️ Environment
- Backend development team in Vietnam  
- JP end user environment (browser cache issues)  
- Internal IT support  

## 📊 Outcome
- End users understood our backend role better.  
- Cache issue was resolved after guiding users to clear browser cache.  
- Internal developers continued to handle direct feedback with end users.  

## 🎯 Lessons Learned
- Importance of emotional control and professionalism when facing unexpected requests.  
- Need for clearer role communication with end users.  
- Evidence (logs, videos) is critical for troubleshooting.  
- Collaboration with IT support teams is essential when translator support is limited.  

## 📌 Next Steps
- Strengthen communication channels with end users to clarify roles.  
- Request proper evidence (logs, screenshots, videos) for future issues.  
- Continue improving patience and professionalism in meetings.  
- Work with IT teams to streamline cache handling and user support.
