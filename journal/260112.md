# 2026-01-12 Log - JP Developer Collaboration

## 📌 Context
Today was the first day working with two Philippine developers from Japan after a 9‑month gap.  
Currently, the team setup in Vietnam includes two developers and one translator. Because the translator is very busy, the two Philippine developers will now work directly with us and receive requirements from the project manager instead of relying on translation.

## 👥 Stakeholders
- Vietnamese development team  
- Two Philippine developers  

## ⚠️ Problem
- The 2 Phil developers from PJ team were initially confused about their roles and tasks.  
- Translator sometimes mixes roles: providing requirements but also asking questions as if from the user, which caused confusion for developers.  
- Communication gaps made requirement confirmation unclear.

## 🔧 How I Handled It
- Asked clarifying questions to align everyone on the same page.  
- Defined roles clearly between developers, translator, and project manager.  
- Practiced patience when requirements were unclear or delayed.  
- Focused on calm communication to reduce misunderstandings.

## 🛠️ Environment
- Team collaboration in Vietnam  
- Direct communication with Philippine developers  
- Requirements flow from end user


## 📊 Outcome
- Philippine developers now understand they will work directly with us.  
- Reduced dependency on translator for requirement flow.  
- Clearer role definition across the team.

## 🎯 Lessons Learned
- Importance of asking the right questions to clarify roles and requirements.  
- Staying patient and calm when communication is confusing.  
- Recognizing that things may not always go as planned, but composure helps maintain progress.

## 📌 Next Steps
- Continue direct collaboration with Philippine developers.  
- Ensure requirement confirmations come from the project manager.  
- Monitor communication flow to avoid confusion caused by overlapping translator roles.
