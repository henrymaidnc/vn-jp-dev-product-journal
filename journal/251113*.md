# 2025-04-24 Log — Lease Quotation Request

## 📌 Context
Received a new request to design **lease quotation** and **dispatch lease quotation**.  
I was given 6 different files, which created confusion about what exactly needed to be implemented.

## ⚠️ Challenge
- Translator was also unclear, which led to duplicated work across all 6 files.  
- Communication gap made it difficult to understand the real requirement.  
- Spent nearly one month working on features that were not actually needed.

## 🔧 How I Handled It
- Discussed with translator to clarify input and output requirements.  
- Eventually learned from users that they only needed **2 outputs**:  
  - Lease quotation  
  - Lease invoice  
- Identified that the lack of Japanese language skills made it hard to clarify requirements directly with users.  
- Recognized that translator alone could not resolve ambiguity, leaving us stuck until users clarified their request.

## 📊 Outcome
- Reduced scope from 6 files to 2 actual deliverables.  
- Avoided further wasted effort by clarifying requirements late in the process.  
- Understood the importance of direct communication with Japanese users.

## 🎯 Lessons Learned
- Always confirm requirements directly with users when possible.  
- Translators are helpful but cannot always clarify technical or business details.  
- Miscommunication can lead to significant wasted effort.  
- Language support is critical for cross-country projects.

## 📌 Next Steps
- Focus development only on **lease quotation** and **lease invoice**.  
- Establish clearer requirement validation process before starting implementation.  
- Explore options for Japanese language support to reduce dependency on translator.
